About Sahulat AI

Making government services accessible to every Pakistani citizen.

The Problem

Millions of Pakistani citizens struggle to navigate government procedures every day. Complex bureaucratic processes, unclear documentation requirements, language barriers, and limited access to reliable information make it incredibly difficult for ordinary people to get essential services done.

From obtaining a CNIC to filing tax returns, citizens often face long queues, repeated visits to offices, and frustration caused by outdated or conflicting information. Urdu-speaking citizens in particular have limited access to digital resources that explain these processes clearly.

Common Challenges:
  • Confusing and unclear documentation requirements
  • Multiple visits needed to government offices
  • Lack of reliable online information in Urdu
  • Outdated fee structures and procedure changes
  • Digital literacy barriers for rural populations
  • No single trusted source for all services

How Sahulat AI Solves This

Sahulat AI is an intelligent bilingual assistant that understands Pakistani government procedures and explains them in simple, easy-to-follow steps. Whether you speak Urdu or English, Sahulat AI meets you where you are.

Simply type or speak your question — and receive clear, structured guidance covering documents needed, fees, processing times, and the correct government office or online portal to visit.

Voice Enabled Urdu & English Text-to-Speech Reliable Information
What Sahulat AI Offers:
  • Step-by-step guidance for government procedures
  • Bilingual support — ask in Urdu or English
  • Voice input for hands-free interaction
  • Text-to-speech for listening to responses
  • Required documents and fees explained clearly
  • Links to relevant government portals where applicable
